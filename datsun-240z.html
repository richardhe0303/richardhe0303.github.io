Datsun 240Z Restoration

A five year mechanical restoration of a 1973 Datsun 240Z, taken from non running to road registered while I studied mechanical engineering full time.

The project covered diagnostics, part sourcing, drivetrain fitment, carburettor tuning, fuel system work, brake and suspension refresh, steering repair, rust repair, interior strip out, and repaint preparation.

Scope of Work Before and After Gallery
Scope

Scope of work

Drivetrain and Engine

  • 4 speed to 5 speed transmission swap with fitment checks
  • Carburettor rebuild and tuning
  • Fuel tank restoration and fuel line replacement

Chassis and Suspension

  • Suspension refresh including most bushings and shocks
  • Brake rebuild with pad replacement
  • Steering rack restoration

Body and Interior

  • Interior strip out and floorpan replacement
  • Rust repair and prevention
  • Full interior repaint
  • Exterior paint and bodywork planned, but vehicle was sold before completion due to relocation
