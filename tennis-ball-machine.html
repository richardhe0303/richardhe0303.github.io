Tennis Ball Pitching Machine

A three wheel tennis ball launcher designed to create realistic shot variation through independent wheel speed control and adjustable pitch and yaw aiming.

Analysis

Why three wheels

Analysis 01

Spin Range Comparison Between 2 Wheel and 3 Wheel Launcher Concepts

This analysis compared a fixed 2 wheel launcher against a fixed 3 wheel triangular launcher at the same ball speed and wheel speed limits. The result showed that the 3 wheel layout could generate a much wider range of controllable spin states, including vertical spin, sidespin, and mixed spin combinations, which justified selecting the 3 wheel concept for the design.

Analysis 02

Fixed Compression Optimization for 3 Wheel Launcher

This analysis swept wheel gap values to find one fixed compression setting that balanced grip, slip resistance, reaction force, and repeatability across representative shot types. A nominal compression of about 5.5 mm was selected as the best compromise because it provided the widest usable operating window without excessive load.

Analysis 03

Motor Drive Ratio Trade Study for Repeatable Shot Delivery

This analysis compared direct drive, reduction, and step up drive ratios using the selected wheel compression, wheel inertia estimate, and pancake motor torque speed data. The result showed that 1 to 1 direct drive gave the best overall balance of usable shot range, motor speed requirement, and launch severity for the proof of concept machine.

Motor and belt drive

  • Three 350 W pancake motors driving 10 inch wheels
  • V belt pulley system selected for smoother torque transfer than shaft couplings
  • Drive ratio selected to support high rim speed while keeping recovery time practical

Pitch and yaw adjustment

Result

What the project proved

Prototype result

Outcome

  • Validated the three wheel launcher concept as the preferred architecture for wider and more controllable spin output
  • Established a working design baseline including wheel compression at about 5.5 mm and a 1 to 1 drive ratio for consistent shot delivery
  • Demonstrated repeatable shot generation across a range of spin types including topspin, backspin, and sidespin
  • Delivered a functional pitching and aiming mechanism with adjustable yaw and pitch range of motion
  • Produced CAD models and technical drawings for over 10 custom components, enabling in house manufacturing and assembly
  • Built and assembled a working prototype using machining, waterjet, and 3D printed parts
