About Me

Richard He

I've been building things for as long as I can remember. Growing up in Melbourne, I was the kid who asked for RC cars, planes, and boats every birthday and Christmas. Dragging broken bikes and 2x4s home from the side of the road like they were treasure, borrowing my dad's tools to tinker with whatever caught my attention, and finding any excuse to be in the garage.

That curiosity is what led me to mechanical engineering. It felt like the closest thing to getting paid for what I'd been doing my whole childhood. I moved to Southern California at 18 on my own to pursue my degree, and while I was there I finally scratched an itch I'd had since I was a teenager: I picked up an abandoned 1973 Datsun 240Z that someone had given up on, and spent five years restoring it from non-running to road-registered. Every weekend, while studying full time. It was the perfect combo of textbook and hands-on. Learning something in class and applying it in the garage the same week. That car confirmed this is what I want to do.

Now I'm back in Melbourne, working across product development and prototyping. I came home because I believe Australia can and should be making more things here, and I want to be part of that. I know there's still a lot for me to learn. I'm looking for a team where I can learn from experienced engineers, contribute where I can, and build things worth building.
