Personal Project

Automatic Rubbish Compactor Bin

A compact household rubbish bin with an integrated automatic compaction system, designed to hold more waste with fewer bag changes and no manual pressing. Built as a small product design project focused on mechanism layout, packaging, fabrication, and iteration.

Problem Mechanism Diagram Result
Problem

What it needed to solve

Design challenge

Household rubbish bins fill quickly with bulky waste. The goal was to design a compact bin that could reduce bag changes without requiring the user to manually press waste down.

Mechanism

How the prototype works

Demo prototype compaction cycle

Compaction system

  • 150 mm stroke linear actuator drives the press plate
  • Dual guide rails keep the press plate level under uneven loads
  • Reverse polarity switch controls up and down motion
  • 12 V DC input with inline fuse for overcurrent protection

Result

What V1 proved

Result

  • Prototype demonstrated automated compaction and validated the overall packaging
  • Dual guides improved alignment, but slight uneven travel remained under load with a single actuator

Technical spec

  • 70 N actuator force
  • 150 mm stroke
  • 40 s full cycle
  • 8 to 11 L adjustable bag volume

Improvement

  • Dual guides improved alignment, but slight uneven travel remained under uneven load with a single actuator
  • V2 will use two synchronized lead screws driven by one motor and belt
  • The goal is to increase compaction force so the rubbish is compressed more tightly, while keeping the press plate level and improving repeatability
